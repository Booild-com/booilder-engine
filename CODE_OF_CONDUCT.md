# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers through the contact listed in
[`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md) or by opening a private report. All reports will
be reviewed and handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and comments that do not
follow this code of conduct.
